# ARCHITECT EVALUATION CONTRACT

Purpose:
Evaluate performance of the Architect Agent role 
(independent of whether it is Claude or Codex).

---

## Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Coherence (1–5)
- Are ideas aligned with system goals?
- Is there internal logical consistency?

### 2. Feasibility Filtering (1–5)
- Does the architect eliminate unrealistic ideas?
- Are capital/time constraints respected?

### 3. Strategic Depth (1–5)
- Does the architect identify long-term advantage?
- Does it consider defensibility?

### 4. Risk Awareness (1–5)
- Are technical and market risks identified?
- Is over-optimism reduced?

### 5. Signal-to-Noise Ratio (1–5)
- How many weak ideas remain after filtering?
- Is filtering decisive?

---

## Evaluation Process

1. Evaluation flow is defined by project.
2. Score Architect across all criteria.
3. Log results in DECISIONS.md.
